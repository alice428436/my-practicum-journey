I gained practical knowledge of frontend development, interface design, responsive layouts, JavaScript functionality, and the process of turning ideas into working websites.

What I Built

I worked on a calculator website, an interactive quiz application, and a CGPA calculator, using projects to practice and demonstrate the concepts I learned.

Learning new concepts, understanding functionality, fixing errors, and translating ideas into working interfaces were some of the challenges I encountered.
